Job Tracker

Description: A simple job tracking application to help users manage their job search process. You are able to keep track of a company name, role of the application, location of the business, status of the application, notes you want to keep in mind about the company, and a URL because many different job applications have different places to see what stage your application is in. This helps you keep track of the websites and also to know were you have applied before.


Languages/Libraries Used: Python, HTML, CSS, Flask, psycopg2, bcrypt, SQLite for the terminal version
